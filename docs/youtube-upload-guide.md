## Recommended Upload Settings

| Setting      | Recommendation                                                                                       |
|--------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Category     | Music, or People & Blogs if the channel is primarily educational                                     |
| Language     | English                                                                                              |
| Audience     | Not made for kids, unless the channel and content are specifically directed at children              |
| Playlist     | `10 Hour Sleep Noise - Black Screen`                                                                 |
| Visibility   | Schedule releases rather than publishing all variants simultaneously                                 |
| Thumbnail    | Use a consistent dark design with the noise color, `10 HOURS`, and `BLACK SCREEN` clearly visible    |
| Captions     | Not required for the audio, but adding a short metadata transcript can improve accessibility         |
| End screens  | Link to the playlist and the nearest alternative color, such as pink from brown                      |
| Mid-roll ads | Avoid manual mid-roll placement because sudden ads defeat the purpose of continuous background audio |
| Comments     | Pin a comment asking which profile and playback device listeners prefer                              |

## Publishing Notes

- Put the exact noise type and intended use near the beginning of each title and the first description sentence.
- Keep `10 Hours` and `Black Screen` visible because they describe strong search and selection criteria.
- Do not title the files as cures for insomnia, ADHD, tinnitus, anxiety, or another health condition.
- Do not claim `no ads` unless channel monetization and YouTube's platform behavior make that promise reliable.
- Use distinct descriptions rather than duplicating one block across every upload.
- Release the tracks over several days and connect them through one playlist to avoid competing launches.
- Keep thumbnail layouts consistent, but assign each profile a recognizable color: cool gray for white, rose for pink,
  amber for warm pink, dark brown for brown, and a split rose/brown treatment for the hybrid.
- Review title and tag performance in YouTube Analytics after enough impressions accumulate. Adjust low-performing
  wording based on actual search terms rather than repeatedly changing every upload at once.
